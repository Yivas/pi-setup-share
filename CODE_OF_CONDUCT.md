# Code of Conduct

Keep issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project discussions welcoming and useful. Critique the work, explain disagreements, and respect people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexual attention, repeated personal attacks, and publishing private information without permission are not acceptable. Do not pressure contributors to share credentials, private profiles, or personal details.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, lock discussions, close disruptive contributions, or restrict participation. Responses should reflect the conduct and its impact; ordinary technical disagreement is not misconduct.

Report abusive or exposed private content through GitHub's reporting controls. A moderation concern that contains no private details may be raised in an issue. Do not repeat private evidence publicly. For a software vulnerability, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

These expectations apply in project spaces and when someone explicitly represents this project elsewhere.
